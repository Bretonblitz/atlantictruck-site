Our story

Cape Breton Built. Cape Breton Proud.

Atlantic Truck & Equipment Repair has been part of Cape Breton's working backbone since 1999. What started as a commitment to keep local fleets moving has grown into the most capable heavy recovery operation east of Halifax. [Add your founding story here — who started the company, how it grew, what makes it yours]

For over 25 years we've been the call that fleet managers make when no one else can handle the job — jackknifed rigs on the Canso Causeway, overturned tankers on logging roads, breakdowns in every corner of the province at every hour of the night. We know these roads. We know these rigs. We show up.

We're proud to be a locally owned, Cape Breton operation. Our team lives here, drives here, and has a stake in keeping Nova Scotia's commercial transportation moving safely.
