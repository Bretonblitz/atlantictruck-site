HOW TO ADD STAFF PHOTOS
========================

Name your photo files exactly as listed below and drop them in this folder.
The site will automatically show the photo instead of the coloured initial circle.

Supported formats: .jpg (also create a .webp version for best performance)

PHOTO TIPS:
-----------
- Square crop (equal width and height)
- Face centred, looking at camera
- In front of a truck or the shop works great
- iPhone photo is fine — just good light
- At least 200x200 pixels
- Filename must be EXACTLY as above (lowercase, underscore between names, .jpg extension)
